Variables in datafiles for meta-analysis of performance measures 

Contains data for all external validations that provide or may provide (subject to review) data for meta-analysis.


lineno - this is used for linking files

author - authors with numbers (e.g Fastner_1) are explained in data_name

year = year of publication

data_name = name of database or if there is none, name of the hospital

id = modelID i.e SHFM or MAGGIC

horizon

outcome = outcome in numerical codes (same codes for SHFM and MAGGIC):
1 ACM
2 ACM or heart Tx
3 ACM or urgent heart Tx
4 ACM or hospitalisation
5 ACM or heart Tx or LVAD
6 ACM or urgent heart Tx or LVAD
7 ACM or hosp for acute HF or urgent heart Tx
8 ACM or ICD shock
9 ACM or urgent heart Tx
10 ACM or LVAD
11 ACM or HF hosp

HF type

acuteHRtype

chronicHFtype

mean_fup = mean followup 
sd_fup = SD of followup 
med_fup = median followup
_25q_fup = 25th quartile of followup 
_75q_fup = 75th quartile of followup
min_fup = min followup
max_fup = max followup 
pyears_fup = person years

sample_n_model = sample size used for the model (ie after any exclusions)
sample_e_model = number of events used for the model (they could be less if complete data used and people with outcomes excluded due to this)

extva_dis_cstat_val = external validation c-statistic value
extva_dis_cstat_lci = external validation c-statistic lower bound of 95%CI
extva_dis_cstat_uci = external validation c-statistic upper bound of 95%CI
extva_dis_cstat_se = external validation c-statistic standard error
extva_dis_cstat_p = external validation c-statistic p-value

extva_dis_auc_val = external validation auc value
extva_dis_auc_lci = external validation auc lower bound of 95%CI
extva_dis_auc_uci = external validation auc upper bound of 95%CI
extva_dis_auc_se = external validation auc standard error
extva_dis_auc_p = external validation auc p-value

OErat_v = O/E ratio value
OErat_l = lower limit of 95% confidence interval for the O/E ratio
OErat_u = upper limit of 95% confidence interval for the O/E ratio
OErat_p = p-value for the O/E ratio

mean1 = mean age 
sd1 = SD of age
median1 = median age
_25q1 = 25th quartile of age
_75q1 = 75th quartile of age
min1 = min age
max1 = max age

female = proportion female

noofpreds = number of PRAISE predictors reported (range 1-14) 
noofpreds2 = number of reported predictors for the Levy 2006 external validation version (range 1-21)
noofpreds3 = number of reported predictors for the Levy 2006 external validation version minus the devices (range 1-18)

indexscore = this variable is to differentiate between the types of "final" model being validated, i.e. nomogram or full model or simplified score

toestD = Initial status of investigation into estimates of discrimination (Estimates not required/Checking other data/Estimates are possible/Exclude)
toestC = Initial status of investigation into estimates of calibration (Estimates not required/Checking other data/Estimates are possible/Exclude)

Sensitivity analysis COVER dimensions:
popul = Participants
casemix = Casemix
indep = Independence of researchers
outc = Outcome 
horiz = Horizon
cal = Calibration
disc = Discrimination 
model_f = Model format and weight 
model_p = Model predictors

anyhighROB = At least one high ROB PROBAST domain 

Subgroup analysis COVER variables:
S1 = Heart failure type: chronic vs mixed vs acute
S2 = By prediction horizon (integer multiples of 12 only)
S3 = Calibration estimate
S4 = Discrimination measure type: c-statistic vs area under the curve 
S5 = By version of model 

Versions are for MAGGIC: Dropped predictors (various, so not a proper subgroup); Original (2 points if HF duration < or <=18mths); Original derivation study; 
Unclear (contradiction); Unclear(maybe a contradiction); Unclear  (reference only); Update (2 points if HF duration > or >=18mths)  
Versions were deduced by: comments by authors; timing of the publication relative to timing of calculation; how the categorical variable of HF duration was reported
in the baseline table or how the authors listed the predictors

Versions are for SHFM: Added BB; Added CRT; Dropped predictors (various, so not a proper subgroup); Levy 2006 PRAISE predictors only; 
Levy 2006 external validation predictors (with devices and drugs and coeffs from other studies); Post-2015 update
Versions were deduced by: comments by authors; predictor lists given by authors; numbers of PRAISE predictors that were reported, 
number of predictors in the external validations of Levy 2006 and how these two numbers compared (if equal=> PRAISE version; if latter is higher =>
Levy 2006 external validation version); post-2015 version was deduced by the combination timing of the publication, comments by the authors and 
whether IABP/Vent/UF were reported at baseline.
